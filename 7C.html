Welcome to Terraria

In Terraria, you are thrust into a world filled with danger. You collect resources and kill enemies to create tools, weapons and armor. There are four main classes in Terraria: Melee, Ranger, Mage, and Summoner. Each class has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the best class for you will depend on your playstyle. This guide is intended to help you choose the best class for you and provide a timeline of progression for each class.

Melee

Melee players use swords, yo-yo's, and other close range weapons. This class is good for players who like fighting enemies up close.
