BRANDING

SKULPT Brand

SKULPT Brand is a Stockholm, Sweden-based branding agency for early-stage startups. The goal for this project was to sow the seeds of a the brand's identity in a slide deck and propose concepts that align and represent the company's values.

Sams Club x UNC ADLOBS

Sam's Club is a well-known and membership-based warehouse chain. For this project, our task was to create a set of ADLOBS, set for a Summer publication, that would encourage a boost in memberships from GenZ consumers. While Sam's Club is known for its bulk grocery and homegoods, the creative team wanted concepts that dive into the membership's other benefits.

Bubbz

Bubbz is new brand of organic, naturally-flavored sodas geared toward more health-conscious consumers. The brand was looking to reach health-conscious consumers, specifically Millennials and Gen Z, who are actively seeking healthier, functional alternatives to traditional sugary sodas.
